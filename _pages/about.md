I am a PhD student in Political Science and International Affairs at the University of Georgia. My major field is American politics, with a minor field in public policy and third field studies in methodology. I hold a B.S. in Political Science from Berry College (summa cum laude).

## Research Interests

- **Voluntary abdication of political powers:** why political actors choose to give up or delegate their own authority
- **Voluntary abdication of civil liberties:** why citizens and institutions willingly trade away their own rights
- **Sincere policymaking vs. position-taking:** when lawmakers try to solve problems versus political signaling
