<p style="margin-bottom: 30px;"> I am a PhD student in Political Science and International Affairs at the University of Georgia. My major field is American politics, with a minor field in public policy and third field studies in methodology. My current research examines the voluntary abdication of political powers, the voluntary abdication of civil liberties, and differences between sincere policymaking and position-taking. </p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 30px;"> I earned a B.S. in Political Science from Berry College, graduating summa cum laude with a minor in music in May of 2025. Before graduate school, I interned at the Lookout Mountain Judicial Circuit District Attorney's office and led voter initiatives at Berry College during the 2024 election cycle. </p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 30px;"> Outside of learning more about politics and statistics, I enjoy playing video games, drawing, and learning new songs on bass guitar. </p>
